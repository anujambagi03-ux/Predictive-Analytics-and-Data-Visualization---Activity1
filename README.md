# 🎓 Predictive Analytics for Academic Probation

## Machine Learning-Based Academic Risk Prediction and Early Intervention

> An end-to-end predictive analytics and machine learning project that identifies students who may be at risk of academic probation, analyzes the major factors associated with academic risk, evaluates multiple classification models, and proposes suitable academic interventions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Academic probation is an important indicator of a student's academic difficulty. Identifying academically struggling students only after they have reached a critical stage may limit the opportunity for timely academic support.

This project applies **Predictive Analytics and Machine Learning** to develop an academic-risk prediction system capable of classifying students into two categories:

- 🟢 **Not At Risk**
- 🔴 **At Risk**

The project analyzes student academic performance together with attendance, previous academic failures, study behaviour, demographic information, family characteristics, and other available attributes.
